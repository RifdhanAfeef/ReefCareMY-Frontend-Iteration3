# Coastal photographs

These photographs represent each island or island group. They do not depict the exact position of every dive site, and do not represent current conditions. Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 28 September 2026, resized to a maximum width of 1280 pixels, and displayed with a cropped view in the regional overview.

- `perhentian.jpg`: [Perhentian Islands, Malaysia, Beach](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Perhentian_Islands,_Malaysia,_Beach.jpg), Vyacheslav Argenberg, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Photograph of Perhentian Besar.
- `redang.jpg`: [Redang Sea Beach](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Redang_Sea_Beach.jpg), Mukherjeesaikat, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Photograph of Pasir Panjang Beach. The resized image retains this license.
- `tioman.jpg`: [Beach of Pulau Tioman](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beach_of_Pulau_Tioman.JPG), Singaporean, [Copyrighted free use](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beach_of_Pulau_Tioman.JPG#Licensing). Photograph of a beach in southern Tioman.
